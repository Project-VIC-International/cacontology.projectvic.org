CAC Asset Forfeiture and Financial Recovery Ontology with gUFO Integration

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/asset-forfeiture#

Definition: An ontology for representing asset forfeiture, property restraints, and financial recovery actions in Internet Crimes Against Children investigations, enhanced with comprehensive gUFO foundational modeling. Based on Criminal Assets Confiscation Taskforce (CACT) operations and Commonwealth financial penalties, featuring gUFO Events, Organizations, Objects, Roles, Phases, and Situations for enhanced semantic precision and anti-rigidity enforcement.
