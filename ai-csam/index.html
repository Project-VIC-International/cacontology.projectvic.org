CAC AI-Generated Content Ontology with gUFO Integration

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/ai-csam#

Definition: An ontology for representing artificial intelligence-generated child sexual abuse material and related detection, investigation, and prosecution challenges. Enhanced with gUFO foundational ontology for improved semantic precision, anti-rigidity modeling, and temporal constraint validation. Based on analysis of Operation Cumberland 2025 and emerging AI-CSAM threats identified by Europol and international law enforcement.
