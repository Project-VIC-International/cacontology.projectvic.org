CAC Sex Trafficking Ontology

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/trafficking#

Definition: Specialized ontology for modeling sex trafficking of minors and related criminal enterprises with gUFO foundational ontology enhancements for improved semantic precision, temporal modeling, organization structure modeling, and validation capabilities. Based on cases like Brooklyn DA v. Eight Defendants (2025).
