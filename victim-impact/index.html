CAC Victim Impact Assessment Ontology

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/victim-impact#

Definition: An ontology for representing victim impact assessments, psychological harm, trauma indicators, therapeutic interventions, and recovery processes in Internet Crimes Against Children investigations, extending UCO and CASE with gUFO foundational ontology enhancements.
