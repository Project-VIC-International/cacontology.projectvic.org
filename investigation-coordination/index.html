CAC Investigation Coordination Ontology

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/investigation-coordination#

Definition: Comprehensive ontology for modeling investigation coordination processes, information sharing, resource sharing, communication protocols, and inter-agency collaboration within CAC investigations. Enhanced with gUFO (gentle Unified Foundational Ontology) integration for improved semantic precision and validation capabilities.
