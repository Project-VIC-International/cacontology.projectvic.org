CAC Specialized Units Ontology

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/specialized-units#

Definition: An ontology for modeling specialized units including K9 detection programs, officer wellness support, advanced investigative capabilities, seasonal operations, and affiliate management in CAC operations with gUFO foundational ontology enhancements for improved semantic precision, temporal modeling, and validation capabilities.
