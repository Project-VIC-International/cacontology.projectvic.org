CAC Recruitment Networks Ontology

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/recruitment-networks#

Definition: An ontology for representing peer recruitment networks, classmate-mediated trafficking, educational institution reporting, and social recruitment patterns in child exploitation cases. Based on analysis of Brooklyn DA cases involving victim recruitment through existing trafficking victims.
