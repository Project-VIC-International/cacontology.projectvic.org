Usa-Federal-Law Module

Namespace: https://cacontology.projectvic.org/usa-federal-law#

Definition: Comprehensive semantic framework for U.S. federal child exploitation and obscenity laws with gUFO foundational ontology grounding for enhanced semantic precision, temporal modeling, and validation capabilities. Based on DOJ CEOS guidelines and models federal statutes, prosecution mechanisms, extraterritorial crimes, child support enforcement intersection, and obscenity law frameworks.
